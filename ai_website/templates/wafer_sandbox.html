Wafer Map Pattern Recognition

During semiconductor fabrication, electrical die sorting generates 2D Wafer Defect Maps (WMAP). Non-random defect clusters are geometric fingerprints of faulty manufacturing tools.

Automated AI classification identifies systemic defects in seconds, preventing entire production lots worth millions of dollars from suffering yield loss.

Semiconductor Yield Engineering Takeaways

Spatial Footprints: Tool faults leave distinct geometric signatures on wafers because fab processes rotate, heat, or polish wafers radially.
Automated Root Cause: Coupling deep vision models with manufacturing execution systems (MES) enables instant tool lockout before scrap accumulates.
Yield & Economics: A 1% yield recovery in a 300mm leading-edge fab represents millions in revenue across high-volume production runs.
Predictive Maintenance: Real-time anomaly alerts enable opportunistic maintenance during scheduled recipe changes rather than emergency fab downtime.
