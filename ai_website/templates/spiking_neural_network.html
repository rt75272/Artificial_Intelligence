This browser-based simulation uses a leaky integrate-and-fire (LIF) model. Each neuron accumulates input current while its voltage leaks toward rest. Crossing the threshold emits a spike, resets the neuron, and sends a delayed synaptic event to connected neurons.

THE NEURON MODEL

Integrate → fire → reset

τ dV/dt = −(V − Vrest) + Iinput + Isyn
  1. Integrate: Incoming currents raise or lower membrane voltage.
  2. Leak: Voltage drifts back toward resting potential over time.
  3. Fire: At threshold, emit a spike and reset; a brief refractory period follows.
  4. Transmit: Connected neurons receive delayed excitatory or inhibitory current.
